## 5. Robust findings (survived every audit)

1. **ICLR submissions titled 'NAME: subtitle' doubled from 2023 to 2026 (20.3% -> 40.5% of 43,482 submissions), on a trend that became convex (accelerated) from about 2022-24, most visibly in 2026.** LPM slope change +2.8 pp/yr [1.3, 4.4] (year level); camera-ready-free pool year-level OR 1.121 [1.019, 1.232]; misclassification-corrected ITS within 10% (V1). *Caveat:* Not a dated 2024 break; not tied to ChatGPT.
2. **A published-only study reproduces the all-submission NAME share, and this survives correction for camera-ready editing.** G_total 1.021 (90% CI 1.001-1.041); editing-corrected [0.949, 1.031] (complete arXiv audit). *Caveat:* A small decision-stage NAME premium (G_dec 1.05) is not identified against editing.
3. **In meaning-fixed title-opener slots, LLM-favoured verbs ('Enhancing' over 'Improving', etc.) rose from 2024 while control pairs did not shift at 2024, including in the camera-ready-free pool; the new openers were added rather than swapped for neutral synonyms.** Pooled post OR 2.18 [1.41, 3.38]; stacked slot-vs-control 2.55 [1.53, 4.25]; NA pool 2.18 [1.28, 3.70]; LLM-variant rate RR 3.91 [1.84, 8.29] vs neutral 1.17 [0.70, 1.94]. *Caveat:* Associational; OR >= 2 depends on 'enhancing'; not sharply dated against 2023; the control null holds at the 2024 specification only.
4. **Title-style markers move more slowly among stayer keys than abstract-topic markers do (style-vs-topic channel contrast).** G_style 0.17 [0.13, 0.20] vs G_topic -0.05 [-0.07, -0.03] (any position). *Caveat:* Declared profile keys, not persons; the slope criterion itself is not diagnostic.
5. **Measured team composition (size x new keys x LLM topic) does not account for most of the 2023-26 NAME rise.** DFL share 0.29 / 0.32 / 0.26 (2024/25/26), far below the 0.50 falsification line; Shapley share net of content 0.09. *Caveat:* The <= 0.30 prediction fails in 2025.
6. **Retitled resubmissions do not add NAME on net.** Mean change 0.000 (se 0.025; 28 adds, 28 drops; n = 297). *Caveat:* Pairs are selected on retitling; a lag behind the population drift is not established.
7. **No title feature's acceptance association survives the camera-ready audit under multiplicity control.** 0 of 18 auditable features pass the Holm gate on 1,311 pre-decision arXiv pairs; NAME DiD +1.48 pp [-0.63, 3.59] vs tipping point 2.92 pp. *Caveat:* 'Not identified' is a statement about the audit, not evidence that editing explains the associations; ALL-CAPS, typography and the em dash cannot be audited through arXiv.
8. **A published-only study recovers mainstream title prevalences within 10% but halves the 2024 NAME slope change and under-represents outsider casing.** M 9/11 within +/-10% (effect scale); ITS break ratio 0.50 [-0.15, 1.16]; ALL-CAPS titles 0.36 [0.26, 0.47] of their all-submission share. *Caveat:* Outsider under-representation is not identified as selection (camera-ready recasing).
9. **Desk rejection and withdrawal deplete 'Enhancing X via Y'-type R24 title words, as they did before 2024. The LLM-style union without R24 is neutral in total, but F24 and the abstract style density show a new 3-4% pre-decision depletion.** R24 pre-decision 0.900 [0.870, 0.931]; camera-ready-free 0.892 [0.850, 0.937]; T4.1 exit OR 0.723 in 2018-23 vs 0.740 in 2024-26. L-union without R24, total 0.998 [0.972, 1.027]. Camera-ready-free pre-decision: F24 0.965 [0.939, 0.993], style density 0.963 [0.957, 0.970]; F24 post/pre 0.914 [0.849, 0.988] against 2018-23 but 0.975 [0.893, 1.071] against 2018-22. *Caveat:* The F24 part is new only against a baseline that includes 2023. No decision-stage association is identified against camera-ready editing (R24's total gain includes an unidentified decision part). Not evidence that review detects LLM use.
10. **The raw withdrawal excess of NAME papers is mostly topic composition.** +4.06 pp cross-section without topic FE; +0.84 [-1.05, 2.74] with topic100 FE. *Caveat:* Within-key, not within-person.
11. **The camera-ready NAME audit, the findability gap and the absence of a camera-ready retitling excess are robust measurement facts about the arXiv audit itself.** Found 86.5% vs 53.1% (accepted vs rejected; OR 5.68); retitling +1.5 pp [-3.2, 6.1]; NAME does not predict findability (OR 1.00 [0.81, 1.22]). *Caveat:* Both retrieval routes share an abstract-similarity rule.
